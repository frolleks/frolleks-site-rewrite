By this point, we know what the "dead internet theory" is. It's where bots and computers make up the majority of internet usage, overtaking us humans. Botfarms have existed for a long time. The recent, rapid advancements in AI has amplifled this theory towards reality. Let's enslave LLMs to mass produce comments and posts. Steering a narrative has never been easier. The dead internet reality is here.

# Let's not move too far. Why can bots even take over?

The ease of automating web requests. I mean, the whole time it's always a program running on your computer to make those requests. *Shocking,* isn't it? Bots run a program that's an ultra minimalistic version of a web browser, it can only make requests to sites/apps it was instructed to. Other than that, it can also be instructed to make massive amounts of requests in a short amount of time.

Some examples would be, of course, spamming Instagram comments with multiple accounts at once.

# What role does AI have here?

A big one. It can do such thing said previously with less human input. You can definitely hook it up to social platforms and generate narratives in realtime with only a couple presses. With it getting increasingly better in capabilities, it can only get scarier from here.

# Why should we worry about it?

With the passing of time, it is getting harder and harder to distinguish between what is real and what isn't. Ever since Anthropic released their [*Claude Opus 5.5*](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5) model, my feed has been bombarded with LLM-generated reels. Some are funny, wild, and what I'm most anxious about is that it can make us... _**FEEL.**_ Let me give you an example:
